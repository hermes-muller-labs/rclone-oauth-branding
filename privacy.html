Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

This OAuth application (“rclone”) is used only by its operator to authorize the rclone program on their computers to read and write files in their own Google Drive.

Data

  • Google account email, as needed to complete OAuth.
  • Drive file metadata and contents the operator chooses to sync or back up.

Tokens are stored in the operator’s local rclone config. This app does not run a public service, does not sell data, and does not share Drive contents with third parties. Google’s processing of Drive data is covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Contact

The support email configured on the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen.
